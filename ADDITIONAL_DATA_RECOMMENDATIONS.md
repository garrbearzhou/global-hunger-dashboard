## High-Priority Additional Data Sources

### 1. **Conflict & Security Data** ⚠️ **HIGHEST PRIORITY**
**Why it matters:** Conflict is the #1 driver of acute food insecurity (per GRFC 2025)
- **Source:** Uppsala Conflict Data Program (UCDP), ACLED, Global Peace Index
- **Indicators:**
  - Active conflicts (ongoing, intensity)
  - Conflict-related deaths
  - Displacement (IDPs, refugees)
  - Years since last major conflict
- **Impact:** Countries in active conflict should have significantly higher vulnerability scores
- **Data availability:** Good coverage, regularly updated
- **URL:** https://ucdp.uu.se/, https://acleddata.com/

### 2. **Food Price Inflation & Volatility** 📈
**Why it matters:** Rapid food price increases make food unaffordable even if available
- **Source:** FAO Food Price Index, World Bank, IMF
- **Indicators:**
  - Annual food price inflation rate
  - Food price volatility index
  - Local food price index (if available)
- **Impact:** High inflation = reduced purchasing power = increased vulnerability
- **Data availability:** Good for most countries
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/worldfoodsituation/foodpricesindex/en/

### 3. **Refugee & Internally Displaced Persons (IDP) Data** 🏃
**Why it matters:** Displaced populations are extremely vulnerable to hunger
- **Source:** UNHCR, IDMC (Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre)
- **Indicators:**
  - Number of refugees hosted
  - Number of IDPs
  - Refugees/IDPs as % of population
  - New displacements in past year
- **Impact:** High displacement = increased vulnerability
- **Data availability:** Excellent, regularly updated
- **URL:** https://www.unhcr.org/, https://www.internal-displacement.org/

### 4. **Food Import Dependency** 🌾
**Why it matters:** Countries dependent on food imports are vulnerable to trade disruptions
- **Source:** FAO, World Bank, UN Comtrade
- **Indicators:**
  - Food import dependency ratio (% of calories imported)
  - Cereal import dependency
  - Food import bill as % of GDP
- **Impact:** High dependency = vulnerability to trade shocks, currency devaluation
- **Data availability:** Good for most countries
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/faostat/

### 5. **Water Scarcity & Access** 💧
**Why it matters:** Water is essential for food production and consumption
- **Source:** FAO AQUASTAT, World Resources Institute (WRI)
- **Indicators:**
  - Water stress index
  - % of population with access to safe water
  - Agricultural water use efficiency
  - Groundwater depletion rate
- **Impact:** Water scarcity = reduced agricultural productivity = food insecurity
- **Data availability:** Good coverage
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/aquastat/, https://www.wri.org/aqueduct

### 6. **Social Protection Coverage** 🛡️
**Why it matters:** Social safety nets reduce vulnerability to food insecurity
- **Source:** World Bank ASPIRE database, ILO
- **Indicators:**
  - % of population covered by social protection
  - Social protection spending as % of GDP
  - Food assistance program coverage
- **Impact:** Low coverage = higher vulnerability
- **Data availability:** Moderate (better for developing countries)
- **URL:** https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/socialprotection

### 7. **Remittances** 💰
**Why it matters:** Remittances are a critical income source for many vulnerable households
- **Source:** World Bank Migration and Remittances Database
- **Indicators:**
  - Remittances as % of GDP
  - Remittances per capita
  - Remittance growth rate
- **Impact:** High remittances = lower vulnerability (protective factor)
- **Data availability:** Excellent
- **URL:** https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/migrationremittancesdiasporaissues

### 8. **Currency Instability & Exchange Rate Volatility** 💱
**Why it matters:** Currency devaluation makes food imports unaffordable
- **Source:** World Bank, IMF, Central Banks
- **Indicators:**
  - Annual currency depreciation rate
  - Exchange rate volatility
  - Inflation rate (general, not just food)
- **Impact:** High volatility = reduced purchasing power
- **Data availability:** Excellent
- **URL:** https://data.worldbank.org/

### 9. **Trade Restrictions & Sanctions** 🚫
**Why it matters:** Sanctions and trade restrictions limit food access
- **Source:** UN Security Council, Global Trade Alert, World Bank
- **Indicators:**
  - Active sanctions (binary)
  - Trade restriction index
  - Agricultural trade barriers
- **Impact:** Sanctions = significantly increased vulnerability
- **Data availability:** Moderate (requires manual tracking)
- **URL:** https://www.globaltradealert.org/

### 10. **Agricultural Land Availability** 🌍
**Why it matters:** Limited arable land reduces food production capacity
- **Source:** FAO, World Bank
- **Indicators:**
  - Arable land per capita
  - % of land suitable for agriculture
  - Agricultural land degradation
- **Impact:** Low land availability = higher vulnerability
- **Data availability:** Good
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/faostat/

## Medium-Priority Additional Data

### 11. **Food Loss & Waste** 🗑️
- **Source:** FAO Food Loss Index
- **Impact:** High loss/waste = reduced food availability
- **Data availability:** Limited (mostly estimates)

### 12. **Dietary Diversity** 🍽️
- **Source:** FAO, Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS)
- **Impact:** Low diversity = micronutrient deficiencies = vulnerability
- **Data availability:** Moderate (household survey data)

### 13. **Women's Empowerment in Agriculture** 👩‍🌾
- **Source:** Women's Empowerment in Agriculture Index (WEAI)
- **Impact:** Women's empowerment = better food security outcomes
- **Data availability:** Limited (specific surveys)

### 14. **Infrastructure Quality** 🛣️
- **Source:** World Bank Logistics Performance Index, Infrastructure Quality Index
- **Impact:** Poor infrastructure = food distribution challenges
- **Data availability:** Good

### 15. **Early Warning Systems** ⚠️
- **Source:** FEWS NET, GIEWS (FAO)
- **Impact:** Presence of early warning = better preparedness
- **Data availability:** Moderate (mostly qualitative)

### 16. **Humanitarian Access** 🚚
- **Source:** ACAPS, OCHA
- **Impact:** Limited access = inability to provide aid
- **Data availability:** Moderate (requires manual assessment)

### 17. **Food Aid Dependency** 📦
- **Source:** WFP, FAO
- **Impact:** High dependency = underlying vulnerability
- **Data availability:** Good

### 18. **Education Levels** 📚
- **Source:** UNESCO, World Bank
- **Impact:** Education = better food security outcomes
- **Data availability:** Excellent

### 19. **Urban vs. Rural Food Security** 🏙️
- **Source:** FAO, World Bank
- **Impact:** Different vulnerabilities in urban vs. rural areas
- **Data availability:** Moderate (requires disaggregation)

### 20. **Food Safety & Quality** 🦠
- **Source:** WHO, FAO
- **Impact:** Foodborne illness = reduced food utilization
- **Data availability:** Limited

## Implementation Notes

1. **Conflict Data Integration:**
   - Add conflict score (0-20 points) based on active conflicts
   - Countries in active conflict: +15-20 points
   - Recent conflict (within 5 years): +5-10 points

2. **Refugee/IDP Integration:**
   - Add displacement score (0-10 points)
   - High displacement (>5% of population): +8-10 points
   - Moderate displacement (1-5%): +4-6 points

3. **Food Price Inflation:**
   - Add inflation score (0-8 points)
   - Very high inflation (>30%): +8 points
   - High inflation (15-30%): +5 points
   - Moderate inflation (5-15%): +2 points

4. **Food Import Dependency:**
   - Add dependency score (0-6 points)
   - Very high dependency (>50%): +6 points
   - High dependency (30-50%): +4 points
