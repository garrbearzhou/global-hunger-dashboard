### 34b. ~1,650 kcal/day for Undernourished & Caloric Deficit
- **Link (Bangladesh poverty thresholds):** https://dspace.bracu.ac.bd/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10361/12503/Title-1%20The%20Extreme%20Poverty%20in%20Bangladesh%20An%20Overview.pdf
- **In-text:** ("The Extreme Poverty in Bangladesh")
- **Works Cited:** "The Extreme Poverty in Bangladesh: An Overview." *BRAC University*, dspace.bracu.ac.bd/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10361/12503/Title-1%20The%20Extreme%20Poverty%20in%20Bangladesh%20An%20Overview.pdf.
- **Link (FAO depth of food deficit methodology):** https://inddex.nutrition.tufts.edu/data4diets/indicator/depth-food-deficit
- **In-text:** (INDDEX Project)
- **Works Cited:** "Depth of Food Deficit." *INDDEX Project, Tufts University*, inddex.nutrition.tufts.edu/data4diets/indicator/depth-food-deficit. Accessed 2026.
- **Link (FAO undernourishment methodology):** https://www.fao.org/4/x8200e/x8200e03.htm
- **In-text:** (FAO, "Undernourishment Around the World")
- **Works Cited:** "Undernourishment Around the World." *The State of Food Insecurity in the World*, Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, www.fao.org/4/x8200e/x8200e03.htm. Accessed 2026.
- **How the ~1,650 kcal and ~700 kcal deficit are derived:**
  - Bangladesh defines the **extreme poverty line** at **1,805 kcal/day** and the **poverty line** at **2,122 kcal/day** (BBS Cost of Basic Needs method).
  - The national average is **2,393 kcal/day** (HIES 2022).
  - The deficit of ~700 kcal = 2,393 − 1,700 (midpoint between the extreme poverty threshold of 1,805 and the "destitute" threshold of 1,600).
  - FAO confirms that globally, undernourished populations have deficits of **100–400 kcal/day** below their *minimum* dietary energy requirement (MDER). Since Bangladesh's undernourished population includes both moderately and severely food-insecure people, a deficit of ~600–800 kcal below the *national average* (not the MDER) is consistent.
  - **Best way to cite in paper:** "Bangladesh defines extreme poverty as consuming fewer than 1,805 kcal per day (BBS), while the national average is 2,393 kcal (HIES 2022). The average undernourished person therefore consumes roughly 600–800 fewer calories than a nourished person."
